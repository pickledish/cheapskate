Cheapskate Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 15, 2026

Cheapskate (https://skate.cheap) is a free, open-source hobby project run by
one person, Brandon Willett.

WHAT IS COLLECTED
- Your name and email address, from Google Sign-In or email sign-up.
- The financial data you enter or upload (accounts, holdings, prices, goals).

HOW IT IS USED
Your data is only used to run the app for you and the family members you
share it with. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used for
anything else. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking.

WHERE IT GOES
- Supabase stores the database and handles sign-in.
- Bunny CDN serves the website.
- If you use cloud statement parsing, the statement's text is sent to an AI
  model through OpenRouter. If you use a local model instead, the text stays
  on your machine.

DELETING YOUR DATA
You can delete your account at any time under Profile & Settings. Data in a
shared family is deleted when the family's last member leaves.
